### Step 2.2: Evaluating Ideas

> We'll use the following framework for evaluating app ideas:
> 
> 1. Mobile:
> How uniquely mobile is the product experience? What makes your app more than a glorified website? Try for 2 or more of these: maps, camera, location, audio, sensors, push, real-time, etc
> 2. Story:
> How compelling is the story around this app once completed? How clear is the value of this app to your audience? How well would your friends or peers respond to this product idea?
> 3. Market:
> How large or unique is the market for this app? What's the size and scale of your potential user base? Does this app provide huge value to a niche group of people? Do you have a well-defined audience of people for this app?
> 4. Habit:
> How habit-forming or addictive is this app? How frequently would an average user open and use this app? Does an average user just consume your app or do they create?
> 5. Scope:
> How well-formed is the scope for this app? How technically challenging will it be to complete this app by the end of the program? Is a stripped-down version of this app still interesting to build?  How clearly defined is the product you want to build? 
> 
> Evaluate each of your top-2 app ideas based on the criteria above.

* **Anime ranking app**
    1. Description: An anime ranking app that ranks anime by ratings and lets you save favorite anime.
    2. Category: Entertainment
    3. Mobile: The app can be useful as a mobile app, as it may send push notifications of anime news and can store favorite anime listings locally.
    4. Story: The story behind the app would be frustration with the UI of other anime ranking apps. Many users have experienced frustrations with similar apps and would be attrated to this one.
    5. Market: The market for the app would be well established, with similar apps existing and a demand for anime ranking apps existing already.
    6. Habit: The app would form habits through allowing users to browse and save anime listings.
    7. Scope: The fundamental features (browsing anime by rank, search and filters, save to favorites) would be simple to implement, but additional features may be added such as news notification or notifications for recently released anime as well as user profiles.
* **People That Are Angry Just Like Me**
    1. Description: An app that accepts a search query and returns links to social media posts of people complaining about the same thing. The app can also have an online community.
    2. Category: Social Media
    3. Mobile: The app will be easy to access if used through a mobile app. The app may also take advantage of photos and push notifs.
    4. Story: The story behind the app would be about poking fun of social media being used to vent about issues.
    5. Market: There isn't a specific market, but many users who have used other social media platforms may enjoy this app.
    6. Habit: The app would be mildly habit forming with the user being able to create a profile, but it may struggle to compete with other social media apps.
    7. Scope: The app may use APIs of other social media websites for search queries. The app may grow in scope based on additional features, like account creation or posting.
